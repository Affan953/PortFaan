About Me

Hello! I'm Affan, a young and passionate Web Developer with a strong eye for UI design. My journey in tech started with a simple curiosity about how websites work, which quickly evolved into a deep passion for building robust and beautiful digital experiences.

I believe that great software should not only function flawlessly under the hood but also provide an intuitive, immersive experience for the user.
